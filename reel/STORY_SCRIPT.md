**Recording tips**
- Record your voice over the **no-music** file, then put the music version (or your own bed) underneath in your editor, so the music sits under your voice.
- The two rounds have a built-in 3-2-1 countdown with sound. Stay quiet during it so viewers can guess.
- Numbers to say: people **1%**, AI **~47%** (same answer to the same blank), held-out **49% → 14%**.
