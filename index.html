🔬 Research lab: Human or AI?

A Mad Libs Turing test. These stories come from published humor research, where real people filled in the blanks and five judges rated how funny each result was. Watch a filled-in story, then guess: did a human pick those words, or an AI?

About the data

Stories and human answers come from the YodaLib release (Garimella, Banea, Hossain & Mihalcea, COLING 2020), which reuses the Fun Libs stories of Hossain, Krumm, Vanderwende, Horvitz & Kautz (EMNLP 2017). Each story has six human answers (three from US players, three from India), each rated 0–3 for funniness by five judges. AI answers are in data/ai_fills.json; add more models with tools/generate_ai_fills.py. Your guesses and answers stay in this browser until you download them.

Write your own Doodle Lib

Write a story and put blanks in square brackets. Every sentence becomes one scene.

How does it know what to animate?
  • [name] names the main stick person (a second [name] brings in a new person).
  • [noun] [animal] [food] [plural noun] make that thing appear.
  • [verb] makes the last thing mentioned do it. If the sentence says “everyone” or “together”, everybody does it.
  • [adjective] [color] right before a noun describes that noun; otherwise it changes the last thing mentioned.
  • [place] changes the scenery. [sound] pops up in a speech bubble or a big comic burst.
  • Want to reuse a word? Name the blank, like [animal:pet], then write {pet} later.
